SNA-SPARSH 2026-27 – How to use this pack

Makes all grant documents for a school, MRC or CRC in a few easy steps.

➡ CFMS Subject Pasting and Reference (open the tab)

  1. Details tab – choose the unit. Pick the Type of grant, the Mandal and the School. Choose “All in mandal” to make the documents of every school in the mandal at once.
  2. Vendor Registration tab – add the vendor. Pick the grant, mandal and school. Type the vendor name, address, beneficiary code and A/C no. (GST no., IFSC and mobile are optional.)
    • Same vendor for many schools? Click “Use this vendor for the other schools…”.
    • Many schools at once? Copy the rows from Excel and paste them in the Bulk entry box.
    • More than one vendor in a school? Click “+ Add another vendor”, then choose the vendor for each work component. To share one component between two vendors, click Split.
  3. Details tab – check the common details.
    • Rc.No. is filled as SPL/SNA-SPARSH/2026-EDN. Challapalli, Gudlavalleru and Pamidimukkala have their own numbers. You can change it.
    • Officer qualification is filled as M.Sc, B.Ed. If the officer's qualification is different, type it in that box.
    • Dates should be in this order: Proceedings → Work Order → MoA → Work Completion → Invoice. A red warning shows if a date is out of order, or if Work Completion is not 5 to 15 days after the Work Order. The Invoice date is taken from the last Payment Voucher of the school (you can change it); other voucher dates are not checked, because payments can be made at any time in the financial year.
    • Work Order no. and Invoice no. are made automatically. You can edit them.
    • DDO code missing? Type it in the “Units without DDO code” list.
  4. Check the Work components. Use the default items, or edit the names, amounts and descriptions. The amounts should add up to the sanctioned amount.
  5. Open the grant tab (School Grant, MRC Grant or CRC Grant), check the documents, then print. Each grant tab has its own Mandal and School boxes and shows the complete pack of that grant in order. Use the Show box to see one document only, e.g. Proceedings or Proc: Vendor Table. To print in batches, fill “Print batch: school no. … to …” (or press Next 10). The Pre-print checklist lists schools with a missing DDO code, unregistered vendor, no vouchers or overspending. Export (Excel) buttons are on the grant tabs, Payment Voucher and Mandal Summary. Note: the sub-component and “Purchase of …” names are suggestions – confirm them against your sanction norms; you can always type your own.
    • Proceedings – the sanction order.
    • SMC Resolution – committee resolution (School Management Committee, Cluster Committee or MRC Committee).
    • Work Order, Work Completion, Quality, MoA, Invoice – signed by the vendor, the SMC Chairman (Composite) or Complex Secretary (School Complex), and the Head Master (MEO for MRC).
    • Utilization and Non-Drawal – signed by the head of the institution only.
    • MEO Acquittance and Staff Acquittance – MRC grant only. Fill the form at the top of the tab.
    • Proc: Vendor Table and Proc: Per Vendor – only for schools with more than one vendor.
  6. Payment Voucher tab. Choose the main component and purpose, then the payee, amount and month. Click ➕ Add to Voucher Register. The voucher total becomes the drawn amount of that component.
  7. Meeting TA. It is paid to the MEO and staff on acquittances. If your STO or DEO says it needs no vendor documents, tick the box in the Details tab to leave it out of the Work Order, Completion, Quality, MoA and Invoice.
  8. Reports.
    • Expenditure Abstract – one page for each school.
    • Mandal Summary – DDO code-wise abstract of a mandal.
    • Drawal Status – how many bills are drawn; tick “Drawn?” when a bill is drawn in CFMS.
    • Voucher Abstract – list of vouchers; can also print the vouchers (2 per page).
    • Vendor Report – vendor-wise expenditure.
    • Work Report – what work is done for every school, MRC and CRC.
  9. Backup / use in any browser. In the Details tab click Save pack with my entries (HTML). Open the downloaded file in any browser or computer and all your entries load automatically. Save it again after new work. Google Sheet auto-save (Details tab) keeps the same entries in a Google Sheet automatically. Export backup / Import backup also works with a small data file.
  10. CFMS Subject & Ref tab. Select the grant and the school in the Details tab, then open this tab. It writes the Subject and Reference text for the CFMS bill – the MEO Rc.No. and date from the Details tab comes first in the Reference, and the Subject is the grant line, e.g. SCHOOL NAME (UDISE)-Rs5000/- SCHOOL COMPOSITE GRANT FOR THE AY 2026-27. The Order (vendor and invoice details) and the Reference are at least 250 characters each. Click Copy and paste it in CFMS.
  11. Print / PDF. Click “Print / PDF” on any tab (or “All”). Choose A4, margins Default, and “Save as PDF”. Any blank line (____) is to be written by hand after printing.
Remember: your entries are saved in this browser only, so use Export backup now and then. “Clear saved entries” in the Details tab removes them. A ⚠ message under the school box means the DDO code or amounts need attention.
